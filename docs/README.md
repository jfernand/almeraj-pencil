their publishers) and not redistributable, so they are linked here, not included:

- Zainab AlMeraj, Brian Wyvill, Tobias Isenberg, Amy A. Gooch, Richard Guy, *Automatically Mimicking

- Zainab Meraj, Brian Wyvill, Tobias Isenberg, Amy A. Gooch, Richard Guy, *Mimicking Hand-Drawn
  Pencil Lines*, Computational Aesthetics in Graphics, Visualization, and Imaging (CAe), 2008:
